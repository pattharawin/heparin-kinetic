How the model works

The model uses baseline and serial PTT measurements to estimate heparin sensitivity and apparent elimination. It then calculates ranges corresponding to the workbook's defined PTT increments.

View workbook equationsR = PTT3 − PTT0
S = R / D
Ke = ln[(PTT180 − PTT0) / (PTT240 − PTT0)] / (t / 60)
Rd3 = 2–2.5 × PTT0; Rd240 = 1.5–2 × PTT0
DL(normal) = Rd3 / S; DL(tight) = Rd240 / S
tdc = 60 × ln[(PTT180 − PTT0) / Rd240] / Ke
Ir = DL(normal) × Ke

Important interpretation notes

  • The maintenance dose is recorded for context but is not referenced by the original workbook's output equations.
  • The workbook labels tdc in minutes; verify the clinical meaning and treatment timing independently.
  • The source uses PTT increments rather than direct total PTT targets; no external clinical target has been substituted.
  • Do not use calculated doses for treatment without local validation and clinician review.
